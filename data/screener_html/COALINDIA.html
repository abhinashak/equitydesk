About

Coal India Ltd is mainly engaged in mining and production of Coal and also operates Coal washeries. The major consumers of the company are power and steel sectors. Consumers from other sectors include cement, fertilizers, brick kilns

Key Points

A Maharatna Company [1] [2] [3]
The company was incorporated in 1972 as Coal Mines Authority Limited and renamed CIL in November 1975 following the nationalisation of the coal sector. It is a Maharatna CPSE under the Ministry of Coal and has 9 wholly owned subsidiaries, 5 other subsidiaries, and 5 joint ventures, including a foreign subsidiary. The Government of India’s stake declined from 63% as of Mar 2026 to 61% in Jun 2026 following the OFS, in which it sold a total of 2% stake.
