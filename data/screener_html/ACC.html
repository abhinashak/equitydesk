About

ACC Limited (incorporated in 1936), a member of the Adani Group, is principally engaged in the business of manufacturing and selling of Cement and Ready Mix Concrete. The Company has manufacturing facilities across India and caters mainly to the domestic market.[1][2]

Key Points

Parent Company[1] [2] [3]
Ambuja Cements, the parent company, is among India’s leading cement manufacturers and ranks as the 9th largest cement producer globally. The board approved the amalgamation of the company with Ambuja Cements in Dec 2025, and the merger scheme is expected to be completed in FY27
