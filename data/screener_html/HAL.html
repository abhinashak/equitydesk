About

Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd (HAL), a 71.64% Government-owned Maharatna CPSE, designs, manufactures, upgrades and provides lifecycle MRO for aircraft, helicopters, aero-engines, avionics/accessories and aerostructures, principally for India’s armed forces.[1]

Key Points

Business Segments
1) Manufacturing
The five-complex model covers:
1) Fixed-wing aircraft: LCA Tejas, Su-30MKI, Do/Hindustan-228, HTT-40 and legacy fleets
2) ⁠Helicopters: ALH/Dhruv-NG, LCH, LUH, Cheetah/Chetak and future IMRH/DBMRH
3) AL-31FP, RD-33 and Shakti engines
4) Avionics, LRUs and accessories
5) Aerostructures, castings and forgings
6) Aircraft, rotorcraft, UAV, engine and airborne-system R&D[1]
