About

IRB Infrastructure Developers Ltd is an infrastructure development and construction company in India with extensive experience in the roads and highways sector. It is also in other business segments in the infrastructure sector, including maintenance of roads, construction, airport development and real estate.[1]

Key Points

Business Profile:[1]
The company is a pioneer and India’s
largest road BOT operator, with 28 projects comprising 18 BOT, 6 TOT, and 4 HAM projects across its parent company and 2 InvITs.
It manages O&M for all projects and owns
the largest TOT project, the Mumbai-Pune Expressway.
Its TOT portfolio accounts for ~43% of India’s awarded TOT market, while the Group has ~12% share of North-South highway connectivity and ~10% of India’s total toll revenue.
Aggregate toll revenue across IRB and both InvITs was ₹8,315 Cr in FY26 versus ₹7,400 Cr in FY25, about 12.5% growth
